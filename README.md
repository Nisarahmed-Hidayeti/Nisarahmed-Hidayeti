## 👋 About Me

Hi! I'm Nisarahmed, a high school student who loves building things.

I started programming in 7th grade, mostly by experimenting and teaching myself. One of my first projects was using Arduino to automate parts of my home, and seeing software interact with the real world is what got me interested in embedded systems.Since then, I've explored everything from electronics and Linux to web development and artificial intelligence. I enjoy creating projects that solve real problems and help me learn something new along the way.

Right now, I'm working on **Phoenix Assistant**, an open-source desktop AI assistant built with Python. My goal is to create a fast, privacy-focused assistant that runs completely on the user's computer and can be customized by anyone.Lately, I've also been building modern websites for local businesses. I enjoy turning ideas into clean, responsive websites that not only look good but also help businesses establish a stronger online presence.

Outside of programming, I'm passionate about astronomy, physics, and technology. I also enjoy playing chess (around 1000 Elo and improving) and solving the Rubik's Cube in about a minute. Both hobbies have taught me patience, logical thinking, and the value of continuous improvement.
